How the Game Works

  • You do not automatically win €10 at the start of the game.
  • You secure €10 only after answering the first question correctly.
  • Each question has 4 possible answers and only 1 correct answer.
  • Questions are presented in random order.
  • Each new correct answer increases your prize according to the game ladder.
  • After answering correctly, you may continue or stop and keep your secured prize.
  • If you answer incorrectly, you lose all the money.
  • You can restart the game after the game ends.
