How the Game Works

  • You start the game with €10.
  • The option to stop and keep your money becomes available after the first question.
  • Each question has 4 possible answers and only 1 correct answer.
  • Questions are presented in random order.
  • Each correct answer doubles your current amount.
  • After answering, you may continue or stop when that option becomes available.
  • If you answer incorrectly, you lose all the money.
  • You can restart the game after the game ends.
